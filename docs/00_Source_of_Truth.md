# THE FIFTH — SOURCE OF TRUTH v0.1

## One-line promise

The Fifth, bir sorun veya farklılığın içinde kişinin kararını gerçekten değiştirebilecek en küçük yeterli ayrımı bulur; kazanç, bedel, belirsizlik ve dış etkiyi bağlama göre görünür kılar ve yeterli noktada durur.

## Why people come

People do not come to “calculate emotions.”
They come because:

- Bir şey var ama adını koyamıyorlar.
- İki seçenek arasında dönüyorlar.
- Birini anlamıyor veya kendilerini anlatamıyorlar.
- Aynı şeyin neden tekrar ettiğini göremiyorlar.
- Daha fazla düşünmek mi, artık kapatmak mı gerektiğini bilmiyorlar.
- Bir kavramı bildikleri deneyimle bağlayarak keşfetmek istiyorlar.

## The experience

The user should not complete a form.
The system should:

1. Offer two main entrances.
2. Generate four adaptive story doors.
3. Ask one low-effort, high-value question.
4. Produce a Reveal.
5. Stop, simulate, prepare, act, or close.
6. Learn only if the result later returns.

## Fixed architecture

### Two entrances
- Bir sorun var
- Bir farklılık var

### Four internal stages
- Notice
- Name
- Smell
- Close / Live

### Four contextual carriers
- Person
- Context
- Relationship
- Society/System

These are internal architecture, not four fields the user must fill.

## The key distinction

General AI can answer when the user knows what to ask.
The Fifth makes the unseen, judgment-changing question visible and knows when not to ask another one.

## 10–20–30–40

These are not truth percentages or intelligence levels.

- 10: concrete signal/story
- 20: hidden weighting and contextual bridge
- 30: provisional judgment and discriminating question
- 40: simulation, closure, or lived outcome

The front-end may feel like 10 → 30.
The engine must preserve the invisible 20.
The lived 40 later creates a new 10′.

## Platform as its own “1”

The platform may have a versioned state:
- current door library
- question ranking rules
- known failure patterns
- cut-off patterns
- language/lens performance
- user feedback aggregates
- expected vs lived outcomes

This state must be updated within explicit ethical and privacy boundaries.
A Custom GPT prototype does not autonomously rewrite this state.
For the MVP, session records are manually reviewed and the Source of Truth is versioned.
A future backend/API may automate safe aggregation.

## What should never happen

- Emotion presented as objective number
- Hidden diagnosis
- Forced compromise
- False balance in abuse/power/harm
- Community majority treated as truth
- Sensitive context inferred as permission
- Every topic opened infinitely
- Paid tier holding safety or basic reasoning hostage
- Employer/partner access to private reasoning scores
- “Level 40” framed as a superior person

## First MVP hypothesis

Compared with ordinary ChatGPT conversation, can The Fifth produce:

- fewer questions
- lower typing burden
- a more discriminating insight
- a more portable final sentence
- lower steering pressure
- a better cut-off

## First test

5 adults × 2 stories:
- one low-risk daily issue
- one medium-risk work/relationship issue

Compare:
- The Fifth GPT
- ordinary ChatGPT
- trusted human conversation

Measure 1–5:
- understood
- new distinction
- no unnecessary depth
- agency preserved
- real-life usefulness

Return after 24–72 hours where possible.
